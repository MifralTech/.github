# Mifral

**Hardware design, verification and validation for embedded systems and dedicated ASICs.**

Mifral is a hardware design organization focused on digital design, RTL verification, embedded hardware validation and ASIC-oriented prototyping. We build educational platforms and hardware projects to make advanced semiconductor workflows more accessible to universities, research groups and technical teams.

Our work connects three core areas:

- ⚙️ **ASIC education and collaborative fabrication**
- 🧪 **RTL simulation and verification**
- 🔌 **Embedded hardware**

---

## ▣ What we do

We design and validate hardware systems across different abstraction levels:

- RTL design and architecture exploration
- Functional verification and simulation
- FPGA and PCB-based prototyping
- ASIC-oriented design flows
- Embedded hardware validation
- Technical training in digital design and semiconductors

Our goal is to help teams move from digital logic concepts to working hardware.

---

## ◈ Projects

### ⚙️ SemiCoLab — Soon

**SemiCoLab** is an educational multiproject ASIC platform for collaborative chip design and fabrication.

It enables students, professors and hardware enthusiasts to design digital logic blocks, known as **TILEs**, and participate in a shared fabrication flow. The project is designed to make the complete ASIC learning cycle more practical: from RTL design to fabrication, PCB-based testing and laboratory validation.

SemiCoLab focuses on:

- Open digital design flows
- Collaborative ASIC fabrication
- Educational semiconductor training
- TILE-based hardware experimentation
- Controlled laboratory access to fabricated designs
